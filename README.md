# Bandit Linux CTF Labs & Performance Monitoring

A curated collection of [Learn SecByte cybersecurity labs](https://learn.secbyte.org/ctf/) featuring Bandit command-line challenges from Levels 7–19 and the Perfmon Sleuth exercise.

This repository helps cybersecurity students, CTF participants, Linux learners, and aspiring security professionals organize practical exercises and track their progress.

## Introduction

Command-line CTF challenges develop Linux familiarity, analytical thinking, and systematic troubleshooting skills. Performance-monitoring exercises offer an additional opportunity to explore system behavior and investigate relevant observations.

Use this index to access the labs, document your methods, and reinforce the concepts you encounter.

## Learning Path

1. Begin with Bandit Level 7–8 and progress through the listed challenges in order.
2. Read each task carefully and inspect the available clues.
3. Test your ideas within the authorized lab environment.
4. Record commands, observations, and explanations.
5. Try Perfmon Sleuth to complement command-line practice with monitoring-focused investigation.
6. Review challenging concepts and revisit exercises when necessary.

## How to Use the Labs

1. Open each lab through its direct link.
2. Follow the instructions and authorized scope.
3. Attempt challenges independently before using hints.
4. Maintain notes describing your approach and results.
5. Review your findings and identify concepts to study further.
